HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from zero to your first paid order.

You bring the customers and set the price. GasFree4you handles the infrastructure behind the order — payment detection, checks and Energy delegation.

STEP 01W

Create a dedicated wallet

Create a separate non-custodial TRON wallet that you fully control. This wallet becomes the payment point for your Energy customers.

STEP 02+

Add it to Sub-rental

Add the public address in your GasFree4you dashboard and set the price your customers will pay for an Energy order.

STEP 03₮

Fund from 10 TRX

Keep a reseller balance in GasFree4you. The wholesale cost of fulfilled Energy orders is deducted from this balance.

STEP 04→

Share the wallet. Earn.

A customer pays your wallet, the order is detected on-chain, GasFree4you fulfills it, and your margin stays with you.

UNIT ECONOMICS

Every completed order has a spread.

You set the retail price. GasFree4you charges the current reseller rate. The difference is your gross profit.

NON-CUSTODIAL BY DESIGN

Your wallet. Your keys. Your money.

GasFree4you does not need access to your wallet. We monitor the public address, detect qualifying payments and delegate Energy. Private keys, signing rights and customer funds remain outside our control.

SERVICE RULES

Automated does not mean uncontrolled.

Orders must satisfy the service rules before Energy is delegated. This protects the reseller flow and prevents obvious misuse.

!

No Energy to sanctioned wallets

If the target address matches a supported sanctions restriction, the Energy order is not fulfilled.

N

Non-custodial reseller wallets only

Use a wallet you control. Exchange deposit addresses and other custodial wallets are not suitable as the reseller payment wallet.

₮

Keep enough reseller balance

An order can only be fulfilled while your GasFree4you reseller balance is sufficient to cover the current order cost.

↕

Reseller pricing can change

Energy market conditions and order parameters can affect fulfillment cost. Profit figures on this page are examples, not a fixed yield.
