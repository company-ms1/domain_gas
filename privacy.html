Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how GasFree4you collects, uses, and protects information when you use our blockchain utility service for TRON Energy delegation.

2. Information We Collect

  • Blockchain Data: Wallet addresses, transaction hashes, and transaction-related amounts submitted through or associated with use of the service.
  • Contact Information: Information you provide voluntarily, such as your email address or Telegram handle when contacting support.
  • Technical Data: Limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and basic usage metrics needed for service reliability, security, and abuse prevention.

3. How We Use Information

  • To process service requests and deliver TRON Energy delegation.
  • To provide customer support when requested.
  • To maintain service reliability, security, and abuse prevention.
  • To monitor performance and improve website functionality.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Information may be shared only in the following cases:

  • When required by applicable law, legal process, or lawful request.
  • When reasonably necessary to detect, investigate, or prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • When needed for basic service infrastructure, hosting, analytics, or technical support.

5. Data Retention

Public blockchain transaction data may remain publicly accessible indefinitely by nature of the blockchain. Support correspondence and related contact information may be retained for up to 12 months, unless a longer retention period is required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

6. Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with the website and to improve service performance, reliability, and usability. These tools may collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and general interaction events.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Responsibility

Users are responsible for the accuracy of the wallet addresses and contact details they provide. Users should also understand that blockchain activity may be publicly visible and traceable by third parties.
