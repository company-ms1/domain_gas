01 — Overview

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how GasFree4you collects, uses, and protects information when you use our blockchain utility service for TRON Energy delegation, AML screening, wallet management, and related tools.

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the service. We do not sell personal data. We do not store private keys or seed phrases — ever.

By using GasFree4you, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the service.

03 — How We Use Information

How We Use Information

  • To process service requests and deliver TRON Energy delegation to the specified wallet address.
  • To run AML and sanctions screening when requested, and to store check results for your reference.
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.
  • To maintain service reliability, detect abuse, and prevent fraudulent or unauthorised use.
  • To generate wallet statements when requested, using publicly available blockchain data.
  • To monitor performance and improve website functionality and usability.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations, including sanctions screening and law enforcement requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

04 — Data Sharing

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Information may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • Legal obligation: when required by applicable law, court order, or a lawful request from a competent authority.
  • Fraud and security: when reasonably necessary to detect, investigate, or prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • AML screening providers: wallet addresses submitted for AML checks are shared with our third-party screening providers solely for the purpose of generating a risk report. Providers are contractually prohibited from using this data for other purposes.
  • Infrastructure partners: hosting, analytics, and technical support providers who process data strictly on our behalf under data processing agreements.
  • Sanctions compliance: in the event of a confirmed sanctions match, relevant information may be disclosed to appropriate authorities as required by law.

Wallet addresses submitted for AML checks are processed by third-party providers. While we select providers with strong data protection practices, we cannot fully control how third-party systems process data once a request is made.

06 — Analytics

Analytics

We use analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with the website and to improve service performance, reliability, and usability. These tools may collect limited technical information including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and general interaction events.

Analytics data is aggregated and not linked to individual user identities where possible. We use Yandex Metrica for website analytics, which may process data on infrastructure located outside the EU/EEA.

By using the service, you consent to this analytics processing. You may opt out by disabling JavaScript or using a browser extension that blocks analytics scripts.

07 — Security

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect stored information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), access controls, and routine security reviews.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and we encourage users to take their own precautions — including using strong passwords and securing their own devices and wallets.

08 — Blockchain & Public Data

Blockchain & Public Data

The TRON blockchain is a public ledger. Any wallet address, transaction hash, or transfer amount processed through or associated with the service may be permanently and publicly visible on the blockchain, accessible to anyone worldwide.

GasFree4you has no ability to delete, modify, or conceal data that has been recorded on the TRON blockchain. This is an inherent property of the technology and is outside our control.

Wallet statements generated by GasFree4you are derived from this publicly available blockchain data. Generating a statement does not create or alter any on-chain record.

09 — Your Rights

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Portability: request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: object to processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at lina@gasfree4you.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe. Note that rights related to blockchain data cannot be fulfilled due to the immutable nature of the public ledger.

10 — Your Responsibility

Your Responsibility

Users are responsible for the accuracy of wallet addresses and contact details they provide. Errors in wallet addresses may result in irreversible loss of funds or failed energy delegation — GasFree4you cannot reverse blockchain transactions.

Users should be aware that all blockchain activity — including transfers made using delegated energy from our service — is publicly visible and permanently traceable by third parties, including analytics providers, regulators, and blockchain analytics firms.
