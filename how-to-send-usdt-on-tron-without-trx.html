Guide for USDT (TRC20) transfers

How to send USDT on TRON without TRX

Now you can rent TRON Energy in the portal and also add 0.345 TRX to the sender wallet to help cover bandwidth. This reduces failed transfers and makes the sending flow simpler.

Open portal Open calculator

What is the main problem?

USDT on TRON requires network resources. If there is not enough Energy or bandwidth, the wallet burns TRX for the fee. Many users want to send USDT but do not want to keep extra TRX on the wallet.

What changed now?

In the GasFree4you portal you can rent Energy for your sender wallet and optionally add 0.345 TRX to the same wallet for bandwidth coverage in one flow.

Who is this for?

For non-custodial wallets such as TronLink, Trust Wallet, Bitget Wallet and other wallets where you control the address and can receive delegated Energy.

Step-by-step: how to send USDT on TRON without keeping extra TRX

  1. Log in to the portal and copy your deposit wallet from your personal account.
  2. Top up your portal balance with TRX. You can do this through an exchange platform or a crypto exchange. In both cases choose the TRX network/asset and send funds to the deposit wallet from your personal account.
  3. Open the Calculator tab and enter the recipient wallet — the wallet that will receive USDT.
  4. The calculator will help estimate how much Energy is needed. As a rule, if the recipient already has USDT, one transfer usually needs about 65,000 Energy. If the recipient wallet has no USDT balance, the transfer may require about 130,000 Energy.
  5. Go to the order block in the portal and credit Energy to the sender wallet — the wallet from which you are going to send USDT.
  6. If you want extra coverage for network costs on the sender wallet, enable the option to add 0.345 TRX for bandwidth coverage.
  7. After Energy is credited, send USDT from your sender wallet.

How to top up the portal balance

The logic is simple: you fund your balance in TRX, then use that balance to order Energy for your wallet.

  • Through exchange platforms: choose the TRX asset, then enter the deposit wallet from your GasFree4you personal account as the recipient.
  • Through crypto exchanges: also choose TRX and withdraw it to the deposit wallet shown in your GasFree4you portal.
  • Important: Energy should be credited to the sender wallet, not to the recipient wallet. The network fee is paid by the wallet that sends USDT.

Why add 0.345 TRX

Energy covers the smart contract execution cost, but a transfer may still need bandwidth on the sender wallet. That is why the portal now offers an additional 0.345 TRX option. It is a practical way to improve the chance of a smooth transfer without forcing the user to manually top up the wallet first.

Why this method is useful

  • You do not need to keep a separate TRX reserve on every wallet.
  • You can estimate the required Energy before sending.
  • You can top up the portal through common channels: exchange platforms and crypto exchanges.
  • You can handle both Energy and bandwidth support in one place — inside the portal.

FAQ

Do I need Energy on the sender or on the recipient wallet?

On the sender wallet. The wallet that sends USDT pays the network cost.

Why do I need to enter the recipient wallet in the calculator?

Because the required Energy depends on the recipient side. If the recipient has no USDT balance, the transfer can cost noticeably more Energy.

Can I top up the portal balance from an exchange or exchange platform?

Yes. Choose TRX and send it to the deposit wallet shown in your GasFree4you personal account.

What does the 0.345 TRX option do?

It adds 0.345 TRX to the sender wallet to help cover bandwidth-related network costs.

Where can I check the required Energy before sending?

In the Calculator tab inside the portal. Enter the recipient wallet there.

Use the portal before you send

Open your personal account, top up the balance in TRX, calculate the Energy requirement for the recipient wallet, then order Energy for the sender wallet and optionally add 0.345 TRX for bandwidth coverage.
