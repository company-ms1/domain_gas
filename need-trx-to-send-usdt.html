Why buy Energy instead of TRX?

When you send USDT on TRON, the network consumes Energy.

If your wallet does not have enough Energy, TRON burns your TRX to cover the transaction cost.

GasFree4you provides the Energy directly to your wallet before the transaction.

Common questions

Short answers for people who found this page after seeing an insufficient Energy or insufficient TRX error.

Do I need TRX to send USDT TRC20?

You need TRON network resources. If you do not have enough Energy, TRON burns TRX. Renting Energy is the alternative.

How much TRX does a USDT transfer burn?

A common estimate is about 6.4 TRX when the recipient already has USDT and about 13 TRX when the recipient has no USDT yet.

How much Energy do I need?

A typical USDT TRC20 transfer needs about 65,000 Energy if the recipient already has USDT, or about 130,000 Energy if the recipient has no USDT yet.

Do I send USDT to GasFree4you?

No. Energy is delegated to your wallet. Your USDT remains in your wallet, and you send it yourself.
