06 — AML & Risk Controls

AML & Risk Controls

Energy recipient addresses are checked against our internal sanctions database, blockchain-related address blacklists and adverse labels supplied by blockchain ecosystem participants. These labels are not necessarily official sanctions designations or records stored directly on-chain.

Service payments and top-ups are subject to sanctions, fraud-indicator and blockchain-risk controls. The depth and timing of checks may vary by payment type, service, risk indicators and customer activity. Checks may occur before or after crediting; enhanced analysis is not guaranteed before every top-up is credited.

Where the initial address check identifies a sanctions-listed or fraud-labelled sender, crediting is automatically blocked. A payment, available service credit, account or API access may be restricted while an alert is reviewed. Blocking credit does not reject or reverse the underlying blockchain transfer.

We may request an explanation, transaction records, evidence of wallet control, source-of-payment information or appropriate identity or business information. Never provide a private key or seed phrase. An unavailable screening provider or failed check is not evidence of low risk.

Screening results and participant-supplied labels are risk indicators, not conclusive proof of wrongdoing. Indirect exposure is not automatically equivalent to a direct sanctions designation. Low risk does not guarantee lawful activity, and a credited top-up does not guarantee approval of every order.

For detailed rules, including prohibited use, restrictions and reconsideration, see our AML & Sanctions Policy. Send review requests to lina@gasfree4you.com.
