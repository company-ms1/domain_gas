02 — Non-Custodial Service

Non-Custodial Service

GasFree4you is a non-custodial service. Users maintain full control of their wallets and private keys at all times. The platform never stores, manages, or has access to user funds, private keys, or seed phrases.

We interact with the TRON network using only the public wallet addresses you provide. No signing authority or custody over your assets is ever transferred to GasFree4you.

Certain services — such as instant energy rental — may involve blockchain transactions executed through service-controlled wallets solely for the technical delivery of the requested service. In these cases, no user funds pass through or are held in service wallets.
