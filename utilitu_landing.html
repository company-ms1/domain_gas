FAQ

Is this a wallet?

No. GasFree is not a wallet and does not store your funds. It helps check, prepare and monitor TRON addresses.

Do I need to enter a private key or seed phrase?

No. Never. A public TRON address is enough for checking.

Can the service access my funds?

No. The service cannot sign transactions on your behalf and has no access to your funds.

Why do I need TRX if I send USDT?

TRON transactions use network resources. If resources are not enough, TRX or Energy may be required.

What is Energy?

Energy is a TRON network resource used for smart contract operations, including USDT TRC20 transfers.

How does Energy rental through a service wallet work?

You open the old Energy rental landing page, calculate the order and send TRX to the service wallet. After payment, the service delegates Energy to your TRON address. The service does not receive access to your USDT.

Why does the Energy button open the old landing page?

The new landing positions GasFree as a wider wallet readiness and operations platform. Energy ordering already works in the old interface, so the button opens the page where the user can place an order now.

Is it suitable for ordinary users?

Yes. You can simply check one wallet before sending USDT TRC20.

Is it suitable for exchangers?

Yes. Teams can work with many addresses, track statuses, run bulk checks and connect alerts.
