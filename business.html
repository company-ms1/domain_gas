WALLET PREPARATION API · EARLY ACCESS

One Endpoint.
Less Infrastructure to Maintain.

Instead of maintaining your own TRON resource management system, integrate one endpoint designed to handle the wallet preparation workflow.

01

Resource Estimation

Determine the Energy required for the intended USDT transfer.

02

Wallet Resource Check

Check available Energy and identify any shortfall.

03

Energy Provisioning

Request additional Energy when necessary.

04

Delegation Confirmation

Track provisioning until the required resources are confirmed.

05

Clear Preparation Result

Return a structured status before your backend signs the transaction.

Designed for real backend workflows.

Target behavior includes idempotent requests, safe retries without duplicate Energy orders, resource sufficiency checks, structured errors, status rechecks, and provider timeouts.

Preparation is not a guarantee of transfer success: parallel transactions may consume resources. Your platform remains responsible for signing, broadcasting, and monitoring transfers.

A FEW MORE DETAILS

Questions,
Answered.

Does GasFree4you send USDT on our behalf?

No. GasFree4you prepares TRON wallet resources. Your infrastructure signs and broadcasts transactions.

Do you need access to our private keys?

No. GasFree4you does not require your private keys.

Do we need to manage Energy packages ourselves?

The planned Wallet Preparation API is designed to handle resource estimation and provisioning through one workflow.

Can we use the API with our existing backend?

Yes. The API is designed for server-side integration with existing applications.

Is the Wallet Preparation API available now?

The universal preparation workflow is being developed. Contact us to discuss early access and integration requirements.

Can we use your existing Energy API today?

Yes. Existing supported Energy operations remain available through GasFree4you.
