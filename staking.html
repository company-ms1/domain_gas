How to connect

After support confirmation and staking in Tronscan, we’ll enable your wallet for payouts.

0) Contact support

Before doing anything, confirm the current Energy demand: @trx2_9_support_bot.

1) Freeze TRX

In TronLink/Trust Wallet, freeze TRX for Energy via Tronscan.

2) Delegate Energy

Delegate the right to use Energy to our service (tokens are not transferred).

3) We enable your wallet

Send your address — we’ll enable payouts. Distributions are daily to your own wallet.

FAQ

Do you guarantee +5.62%?
No. +5.62% is a target add-on and may be revised; it depends on Energy demand and market conditions. No guarantees of performance or results.
Do I need to stake right away?
No. Contact support first and confirm current Energy demand. Only after confirmation proceed to staking/delegation.
Where do I stake?
In the official TRON interface — Tronscan.
How are payouts made?
Payouts are credited daily to your own wallet (the one used for staking/delegation).
Do my TRX remain with me?
Yes. Tokens are not transferred — you delegate only the right to use Energy. You control your assets.
