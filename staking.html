How to connect

After support confirmation and staking in Tronscan, we’ll enable your wallet for payouts.

0) Contact support

Before doing anything, confirm the current Energy demand: @trx2_9_support_bot.

1) Freeze TRX

In TronLink/Trust Wallet, freeze TRX for Energy via Tronscan.

2) Delegate Energy

Delegate the right to use Energy to our service (tokens are not transferred).

3) We enable your wallet

Send your address — we’ll enable payouts. Distributions are daily to your own wallet.
