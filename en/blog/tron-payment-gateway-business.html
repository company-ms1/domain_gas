Accepting USDT for online stores: a TRON payment service

Build a USDT TRC-20 payment service and use Energy rental to manage the cost of merchant settlements.

From a wallet address to a payment product

Adding a wallet address to a checkout page is easy. A store also needs to know which order was paid, whether the amount is correct, when to release goods and how to refund a customer. Manual checks become inconvenient as payments grow.

A USDT TRC-20 payment service creates payment requests, tracks incoming transfers, notifies the merchant and keeps an operation history. Revenue can come from subscriptions, processing fees or paid integrations and reporting.

Which transfers can become cheaper

Distinguish incoming payments from outgoing operations. Receiving USDT does not itself mean the service pays for the buyer’s transfer. Its own network costs arise when moving funds from deposit addresses, settling with merchants or issuing refunds.

Energy rental can reduce costs for these outgoing transfers. Resources must be supplied to the addresses actually sending the funds. Design the payment and fund movement flow before development: unnecessary transfers add costs.

What the merchant gains

Savings can support more affordable settlements, a lower withdrawal threshold or a competitive service fee. Choose the settlement frequency using the full cost of moving funds, including rental, Bandwidth and operating expenses.

Merchants buy convenient checkout and predictable access to revenue. Start with an integration for one commerce platform and clear order reconciliation. Managing Energy helps maintain attractive terms as transaction volume grows.

How to calculate the benefit of Energy rental

USDT TRC-20 transfers consume Energy and Bandwidth. If resources are insufficient, TRX is burned to cover the shortfall in fees. Rental provides access to Energy through delegation from another account; the resource is needed at the sending address.

Benefit over a period = Energy costs without rental − rental price − additional rental management costs. Compare the same transaction volume under current terms and include unused rented Energy. Account for Bandwidth and other possible expenses separately.

Sources and services: TRON · Resource model, TRON · Paying for resources, TRON · Delegating resources.

Does renting Energy guarantee the same savings for every business?

No. Results depend on rental price and duration, available resources and actual transaction consumption. Compare the full cost for your transaction flow before using demonstrated savings to set prices.
