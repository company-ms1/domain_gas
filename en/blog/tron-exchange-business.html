An online USDT TRC-20 exchange: competing through lower operating costs

How a TRON exchange earns revenue and how Energy rental can help offer customers better terms.

Build a business around USDT exchange

Customers choose an online exchange for its rate, speed and trustworthiness. The owner has another question behind every order: what remains after expenses? One business idea is an exchange supporting USDT on TRON. Start with a limited set of currency pairs and a specific audience, then expand as volume grows.

Revenue comes from fees or the difference between buying and selling rates. It must cover liquidity, payment partners, support, transaction checks and network costs.

Where Energy rental changes the economics

When an exchange regularly sends USDT to customers, every outgoing transfer becomes part of the cost of fulfilling an order. This is especially noticeable on small exchanges, where network costs can absorb a substantial share of earnings.

Energy rental can be integrated into the payout process. Estimate the resources needed, rent the missing Energy for the sending address, confirm that it is available and then send the transfer. Suitable rental terms can reduce outgoing transaction costs.

Turn savings into a competitive advantage

Savings can fund a better exchange rate, a lower minimum order or additional margin for development. Measure profit per completed order after all expenses, rather than just the spread between rates.

The advantage emerges when an exchange offers customers better terms while remaining profitable. Managing Energy becomes part of its operating model alongside sourcing liquidity and automating order processing.

How to calculate the benefit of Energy rental

USDT TRC-20 transfers consume Energy and Bandwidth. If resources are insufficient, TRX is burned to cover the shortfall in fees. Rental provides access to Energy through delegation from another account; the resource is needed at the sending address.

Benefit over a period = Energy costs without rental − rental price − additional rental management costs. Compare the same transaction volume under current terms and include unused rented Energy. Account for Bandwidth and other possible expenses separately.

Does renting Energy guarantee the same savings for every business?

No. Results depend on rental price and duration, available resources and actual transaction consumption. Compare the full cost for your transaction flow before using demonstrated savings to set prices.
