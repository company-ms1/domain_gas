02 — Service Description

Service Description

GasFree4you provides temporary delegation of TRON Energy to wallet addresses in exchange for TRX payment. The service allows users to cover USDT-TRC20 transfer fees using delegated energy rather than burning their own TRX at the standard network rate.

Once energy is delivered, it is available for the stated rental period and cannot be recalled or reversed early. Energy delegation is executed on-chain and is final upon delivery.

In addition to energy delegation, the platform provides complementary tools: AML and sanctions screening, multi-wallet dashboard, wallet statement generation, energy calculator, and a referral program. Each tool is governed by these Terms unless a separate agreement is specified.
